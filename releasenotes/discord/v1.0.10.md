## Stimma 1.0.10 is here

**Masks are editable now.** They used to be a one-time choice; now every adjustment and generative edit has an expandable Mask item in the edit stack you can go back into. A mask can hold several named selections combined with Add, Subtract or Intersect — toggle one off, delete it, or hover it to see exactly what it contributes. Subject, sky, color, brush, lasso, marquee and gradient selections all keep the info that made them instead of flattening into anonymous pixels, and gradients keep their live handles so you can re-drag them later and watch the adjustment update.

**Repaint** — generative edits are an iterative loop now. Change the mask or the description, hit Re-run, and get a fresh batch of candidates without throwing away the old ones. Every batch works from the same original base, so repeated experiments don't compound or degrade the image.

Also:
- Sky selection is noticeably cleaner around clouds, foliage, buildings and the horizon
- The editor adapts to the space beside the Edits panel — labels collapse to icons, nothing overlaps on narrow layouts
- Fixed scoped adjustment masks drifting out of position in compact layouts
- Better loading and selection indicators on tool results

Existing editor documents render exactly as before — their old single masks only get upgraded once you start editing their structure.
