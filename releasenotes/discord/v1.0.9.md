## Stimma 1.0.9 is here

**The image editor is rebuilt, and it's non-destructive.** Every edit is a step in a stack you can reorder, hide, re-open or delete — nothing is baked in until you save. Six tool families: Crop, Retouch, Generate, Paint, Adjust and Annotate. Selection is always available (marquees, lasso, magic wand, brush, object-select by name, re-draggable gradients) and combines with add/subtract/intersect. Generate hands part of the picture to a model — remove object, remove background, regenerate from a description, expand the canvas — and only pixels inside your selection change.

**SVG is a first-class document type.** Draw and revise icons, logos and wordmarks in chat as versioned artifacts, then export source, PNG at any size, vector PDF, embeddable code, or full app icon bundles for macOS/Windows/iOS/Android/web.

Also landing:
- Topaz Labs (8 tools), FLUX Erase + Outpainting, P-Image and Bria upscalers, inpainting on every Nano Banana and Flux.2 tier
- Four local image tools built on ComfyUI-Darkroom: Film Stock, Develop, Color Grade, and Lens & Optics with 102 real lens profiles
- ComfyUI-Stimma: MiniMax H3 video with synced stereo audio, RMBG 2.0, Flux.2 Klein outpainting, LTX-2.3
- Rebuilt tool-screen mask editor with prep controls (flip, rotate, crop, scale, extend) — extend then generate = outpaint
- Model choice splits into four per-profile roles with its own reasoning-effort control, and every model tells you whose balance it spends
- One window per profile, browser-style
- New app icon, incl. a Liquid Glass one on macOS 26

...and a long tail of fixes, including chats becoming unusable after deleting mid-conversation, transcripts going stale after a reconnect, and slideshow videos going black on return.
