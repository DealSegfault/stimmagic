## Stimma 1.0.11 is here

Prompt enhancement now writes in MiniMax H3's task-specific audiovisual format across text, image, first/last-frame and reference-to-video workflows. It uses the duration, audio setting and reference media you actually picked, keeps dialogue and on-screen text you asked for, and stops inventing dialogue and music you didn't. Reference-to-video takes clearly numbered picture, video and audio references — mixed types included — and checks there's at least one usable one. The references you can see are the ones that get sent; stale hidden frame selections can't override them anymore.

Video review on the Tool stage:
- Clips loop silently. Select one and it plays a single full sound audition, then goes back to the muted loop.
- The speaker control replays or stops an audition, shows progress, and has adjustable audition volume. `M` auditions the staged clip.
- Keyboard nav only affects the stage you're looking at, so arrowing through one tool's results won't move cached stages in the background.
- Slideshow volume controls open downward when there's no room above.
