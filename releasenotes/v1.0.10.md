# Release Notes

## Editable Masks

- Masks on adjustments and generative edits are no longer a one-time choice. Every affected step now has an expandable Mask item in the edit stack, where you can return to the selection, see how it was built, and continue editing it.
- A mask can contain several named selections combined with Add, Subtract, or Intersect. Toggle a component off, delete it, select it from the stack, or hover it to preview exactly what it contributes.
- Rectangle, ellipse, lasso, subject, color, sky, brush, and gradient selections retain the information that made them. Combining a subject selection with a marquee, for example, keeps both as editable components instead of flattening them into anonymous pixels.
- Linear and radial gradients keep their live handles. Re-open the mask later and drag the gradient into place while watching the adjustment update.
- Soft edges remain soft all the way through the composed mask. Masks also stay aligned when you crop, reorder edits, save, close, and reopen the image.
- Existing editor documents continue to render as before. Their older single masks are upgraded only when you begin editing their structure.

## Repaint

- Generative edits now support an iterative Repaint loop. Change the mask, description, or other controls and choose Re-run to add a fresh batch of candidates without throwing away the previous results.
- New candidate batches use the same original base, so repeated experiments do not accumulate changes or degrade the image. You can move between batches and keep the result you prefer.
- While editing a generative mask, the selection outline shows the effective composed mask directly on the canvas. Deselect or Escape finishes the mask session; choosing New starts a separate selection.
- Stimma warns when the revised mask reaches beyond the area covered by the generated sample, helping catch edits that may not have enough replacement pixels before you commit them.

## Better Sky Selection

- Sky selection has been refined for cleaner boundaries around clouds, foliage, buildings, and the horizon. It preserves finer detail and produces a more useful starting mask for targeted color, light, and generative edits.

## Editor Polish

- The editor now adapts to the actual space left beside the resizable Edits panel. On narrow layouts, tool labels collapse to icons, model names truncate cleanly, and controls wrap without overlapping one another.
- Generate controls remain aligned when they move onto a second row, and Expand Canvas switches between a row and a compact grid according to the room it really has.
- Fixed scoped adjustment masks shifting out of position in compact layouts.
- Improved loading and selection indicators on tool results so the active item and in-progress work are easier to distinguish.
