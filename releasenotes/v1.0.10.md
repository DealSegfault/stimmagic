# Release Notes

## Image Editor

- Masks on adjustments and generative edits are now fully editable. Expand a step's Mask to inspect, combine, toggle, or remove its selection components, including soft masks and live gradient handles.
- Repaint keeps generative edits iterative: refine the mask, re-run the edit, and compare new candidate batches without rebuilding the step.
- Sky selection now separates fine details more accurately for cleaner edits around clouds, trees, and skylines.
- Compact editor layouts stay usable as the Edits sidebar changes width: toolbars collapse cleanly, model names truncate, and expand controls reflow without overlapping.
- Fixed mask placement on compact adjustments and improved loading and selection indicators on tool results.
